Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Educed Moment is an independent game studio operated by Tristen MacPherson in Saskatoon, Canada. This policy covers the games published by Educed Moment, including those hosted on third-party platforms such as Poki.

What we collect

Our games do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address, or other personal details.

  • Multiplayer connections. When you play a multiplayer game, your device connects to our game server (hosted on Fly.io). Your IP address is processed transiently to route game traffic and establish the connection. It is not stored beyond what is needed to operate the session, and it is not used to identify or profile you.
  • Local save data. Game progress is stored locally on your device (for example, in browser storage). It is not transmitted to us.
  • Optional in-game text. Some games may offer optional features where you can submit short text (for example, feedback or journal-style entries). These are only sent to us if you explicitly consent in-game, and we ask that you avoid including personal information in them.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not run our own advertising, tracking, or analytics networks.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party platforms

When you play our games on a platform such as Poki, that platform may collect data (for example, for advertisements or platform analytics) under its own privacy policy. See the Poki privacy statement for details. This policy covers only the data handled by Educed Moment.

Data retention and your rights

Because we collect so little, there is usually nothing to delete. If you have submitted optional in-game text and would like it removed, or have any question about this policy, contact us and we will respond promptly.
